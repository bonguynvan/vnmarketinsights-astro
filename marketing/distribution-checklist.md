# Distribution Checklist — 0-Outreach Findability

**Goal:** get VN Market Insights discovered by foreign operators selling into Vietnam, without cold-pitching anyone. Two engines: **(A) directories** = backlinks + AI-citation signal; **(B) communities** = answer real questions where buyers already are.

**Golden rules**
- Write ONE canonical 1-line description and reuse it everywhere (keeps your "entity signal" consistent for Google + AI):
  > *VN Market Insights — free, sourced datasets and decision tools (landed-cost, PSP fees, channel payout) for foreign brands selling into Vietnam.*
- In communities: **answer the question first, tool as a footnote.** Never drop a bare link. 1 helpful link per post max.
- Add `?utm_source=<site>` to links so you can see in analytics what actually works.
- Prioritize by fit + authority. 20 relevant listings beat 100 random ones.
