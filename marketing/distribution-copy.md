## 5. Quora answers

### Q: "What are the import duties when selling into Vietnam?"

> Vietnam import duty runs **0–30% depending on the HS code**, charged on the CIF value, **plus 10% VAT** on top, and some categories carry special consumption tax. The big recent change: the **low-value de-minimis exemption was removed on 18 February 2025**, so even small parcels that used to enter duty-free now get taxed. In practice, cross-border landed cost ends up ~30–50% higher than goods held in local inventory.
>
> I maintain a free calculator that computes duty + VAT + total landed cost with the sources shown, if you want to model a specific product: [link]. (Not tax advice — confirm your HS code with a customs broker.)

### Q: "Can a foreigner sell on Shopee / TikTok Shop in Vietnam?"

> Yes — you can sell cross-border on Shopee, TikTok Shop, and Lazada **without a local Vietnamese entity**, which is how most foreign brands test the market (80–90% of early revenue comes through marketplaces). You only need a WFOE (100% foreign-owned LLC) once you want to hold local inventory for faster delivery or run your own DTC site. TikTok Shop has grown to ~41% market share, so it's usually worth being on at least two platforms.
>
> The economics differ by platform (commission + ad rates + Decree 117 tax withholding), so it's worth comparing net payout before you commit. I have a free tool that does that comparison: [link].
