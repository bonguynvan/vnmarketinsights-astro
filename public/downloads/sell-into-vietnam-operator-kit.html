1 · Landed-cost cheat-sheet

Vietnam taxes imports on the CIF value (cost + insurance + freight). Since 18 February 2025 there is no low-value de-minimis exemption — every parcel is taxed.

CIF = goods value + international shipping + insurance
Import duty = CIF × duty rate (0–30%+ by HS code)
VAT = (CIF + import duty) × 10%
Total landed cost = CIF + import duty + VAT
Worked example. Goods $100 + shipping $15 = CIF $115. Apparel duty 20% → $23. VAT 10% of $138 → $13.80. Total landed cost $151.80 — an effective 32% tax on CIF. Price this in before you set a shelf price.

*Rates are HS-code specific — treat these as directional. An FTA (CPTPP, EVFTA, RCEP, ATIGA) with a valid Certificate of Origin can cut duty, often to 0%. From 1 July 2025, e-commerce platforms also withhold tax per order (goods: 1% VAT + 0.5% personal income tax for resident sellers, 1% for non-residents). 2 · Which channel? (2025 marketplace shares)

Vietnam's four-platform marketplace GMV hit VND 429.7 trillion (~$16.4B) in 2025, up 34.75% on 3.94 billion products — but the market has consolidated onto two platforms.

TikTok Shop~41%Up from ~29% in 2024; wins video/livestream categories (fashion, beauty, FMCG)

Rule of thumb. Price-led general merchandise → Shopee. Demonstrates well on video → TikTok Shop (its sellers grew ~96% to ~267,000 in H1 2025). Authorized brand wanting trust signals → Lazada/LazMall. Most serious sellers list on at least two.

3 · E-Commerce Law 2025 — compliance checklist

The new E-Commerce Law (No. 122/2025/QH15) takes effect 1 July 2026 and tightens the rules for foreign platforms and sellers. Get ahead of it:

  • Appoint a local representative in Vietnam (foreign sellers/platforms)
  • Complete VNeID seller verification (electronic identity)
  • Budget for platform deposits and accept joint liability terms
  • Register for tax; confirm the platform's per-order withholding under Decree 117/2025
  • Decide your entry path: cross-border (validate demand cheaply) → local entity (IRC → ERC, or ERC-first under Law 143/2025) once volume justifies it
  • For F&B/cosmetics/supplements: add product-specific licensing (food-safety certificate, etc.)
