# Welcome email — "Sell-Into-Vietnam Operator Kit"

The email that fires from your Kit automation (trigger: added to tag `sell-into-vietnam-kit`).
Written in a personal, founder-to-reader voice so it reads real, not automated. Fill in every
`[bracket]` with your own details, then paste the body into the Kit email editor.

---

## Before you paste — 2 quick settings so it looks real

1. **From name** (Kit → Settings → Email → sender details): set it to a person, not a bare address —
   e.g. **`Bo (VN Market Insights)`**. Recipients see a name they can trust.
2. **Reply-to**: keep it as an inbox you actually read (replies are gold for a new list).
3. *(Optional, later)* Add a **custom sending domain** so mail comes from `hi@vnmarketinsights.com`
   instead of a gmail address — much better deliverability + credibility. Kit → Settings → Email →
   sending domain (needs a few DNS records on vnmarketinsights.com). Not required to launch.
