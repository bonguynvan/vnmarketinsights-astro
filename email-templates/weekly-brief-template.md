# Weekly brief — template + a ready-to-send first issue

Send this as a **Broadcast** in Kit (Kit → Broadcasts → New) to your confirmed subscribers.
Keep it short and consistent — 3 items, one link. A dependable short brief beats a rare long one.

## How to send
1. Kit → **Broadcasts** → **New broadcast**.
2. Recipients: all **confirmed** subscribers (or a segment later).
3. Paste the body, set the **From name** to a person (`Bo (VN Market Insights)`), from `bo@vnmarketinsights.com`.
4. Send a **test to yourself** first, check the links, then send.
5. Cadence: weekly-ish. If a week is thin, **skip it** rather than padding.

## Subject-line pattern
`Vietnam market brief — [one hook]` — e.g. `Vietnam market brief — TikTok Shop's 41%`

## Structure (reuse each week)
- 1–2 line intro (personal, founder voice)
- **3 items**, each: a bolded headline + one or two sentences + a number + the source
- One link/tool CTA
- Short sign-off
